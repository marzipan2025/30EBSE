Hello.

30EBSE is a reader that shows one sentence per page.

# Turning pages

Tap the wide area at the bottom of the screen to go to the next sentence.

Tap the narrow area at the bottom left to go back one sentence.

You can also swipe from left to right to go back.

# UI

Tap anywhere in the upper part of the screen to show the UI.

Tap or drag the bar at the bottom to jump straight to any point.

Tap ↰ at the top left to return to the list.

The UI hides by itself after 5 seconds without a touch.

# Screen refresh

On e-ink devices, a time notice briefly appears on the hour, and at 15, 30 and 45 minutes past.

At that moment the screen flashes black once to clear ghosting.

# Connecting Google Drive

Make a folder in Google Drive and put the texts you want to read in it.

It reads Google Docs, pdf, docx, txt, epub, md and srt.

Set the folder's sharing to "Anyone with the link" and copy the link.

Tap ○ at the top right of the list, paste the link and tap Save.

Tap a title in the list to download and open it.

If you add more files to the folder, tap ↩ in the list.

# Cautions

Anyone who has the link can see the folder.

Put in only texts that are fine to be public.

Saving a different link erases the texts and reading positions from the previous folder.

Work or school accounts may not allow link sharing.

When a new version of the app is out, you'll be told when you tap ↩.

Happy reading.
